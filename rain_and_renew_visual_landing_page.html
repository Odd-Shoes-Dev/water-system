02 / A practical answer

Now bring the water closer.

Rain falls on every roof. A tank can keep it for the days when it doesn't.

Rain&Renew installs rainwater harvesting systems at households in water-insecure communities. The first two pilot tanks hold 2,000 litres and 1,000 litres.

Water used for washing doesn't have to be wasted either. Treated greywater can be reused for non-drinking purposes, so every drop works twice.

07 / The person behind the pilot

It started with one person and two tanks.

Arinda Catherine, known as Cathy, founded Rain&Renew and manages the pilot herself.

She also works with Youth Fraternity for Change Uganda (YFC) in western Uganda, where she sees how safety, education and opportunity are limited for women and young people. Rain&Renew is her own initiative.

She did not wait for a perfect plan. She began with what she could do, and she is learning from each step before she scales up.

Why there is no price list yet

Current indicative costs per household are about UGX 1.5 million for a 2,000-litre rainwater harvesting system and about UGX 1.8 million for a greywater recycling system. The right mix differs for every household, and schools and hospitals need separate assessment. Cathy to confirm before publication

The full budget will also cover training, installation differences, maintenance, monitoring, river activities and staffing. We will publish it before asking for a specific amount, and we will never claim a donation covers more than it does.

Are the results verified?

The figures are reported by Cathy and are approximate. Each is shown with its reporting period, and litres are cumulative totals, not stored water.

Is the recycled water safe?

Greywater is treated and reused only for non-drinking purposes. Systems are designed and maintained with health and safety in mind.

Is Rain&Renew part of YFC?

No. Cathy works with YFC, and that experience informs her work, but Rain&Renew is her own initiative. Confirm any formal relationship before launch

Has the river programme started?

Not yet. The reporting tool and clean-ups are planned for the next phase, and we label them that way everywhere.
